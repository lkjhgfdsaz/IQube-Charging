💡 Smart Tips

  • Eco Mode full-range reference: 115 KM.
  • Power Mode full-range reference: 76 KM.
  • Battery percentage is common to both modes.
  • Eco/Power mode changes the estimated range, not the battery percentage.
  • Use your actual daily distance for better predictions.
  • The planner does not force a charge simply because the battery is above the reserve.
  • Charging cost is an estimate based on battery capacity, charging efficiency and tariff entered above.
